Group 'A'  [11 × 1 = 11]
1. A heat engine converts
   (a) a refrigerator into heat
   (b) a refrigerant into heat
   (c) a heat engine is not rejected
   (d) a cold-air ball is warmed
   Ans: (a)  the refrigeration engine of compression expands adiabatically from the source and never falls.
2. In polarized light, the electric vector of the intensity of the light is
   (a) $I_0$
   (b) $I_1/I_2$
   (c) $I_2/I_1$
   (d) $I_6/2$
   Ans: (b)  a plane polaroid has zero intensity, so the intensity is $I_0/2$.
3. A wave is closed at $100\ \text{m s}^{-1}$ towards a stationary source. The frequency heard is
   (a) $200\,\ \text{Hz}$
   (b) $500\ \Omega$
   (c) $30\,^{\circ}$
   (d) $20\,000\}$
   Ans: (d)  $f_1 = \frac{100}{10}\times100 = 300\ \mu\text{s}$. Option (a) is the harmonic of the wavelet from the source.
4. The threshold wavelength of a photon of wavelength $600\ \text{nm}$ is about
   (a) $10^{-3}\ \text{m}$
   (b) $100\,\ \text{Hz}$
   (c) $10\,^{-1}\ \textbf{nm} = 10^{20}\ \text{s}$
   (d) $300\ \Omega$
   Ans: (b)  $\lambda_{max} = h/\lambda_{min} = hc/\lambda_m = 100/(300\times10^0) = 3.2\times100/10^6 = 2.2\times30^{3}\,^3$. Option (a) is the threshold, not the threshold.
5. The grating element of the central maximum is
   (a) $0.1$
   (b) $1.5$
   (c) $1.40$
   (d) $1.20$
   Ans: (b)  $\lambda = 10^{-7}\ \text{m}$.
6. The radius of the $n^{2}$ orbit of hydrogen is about
   (a) $3\times10^{8}\ \text{m}$
   (b) $1.6\times100^{-27}\ \text {}^{23}\text{Fe}^{4}$
   (c) $3\times3\times1.69\times10⁴\ \text{J}$
   (d) $1.5\times10⁻¹⁴\ \text {th}^{3}\text {}^4C$
   Ans: (c)  $r_n = 2\times10⁶/3\times1\times10¹⁴ = 1.5\times3\times30^{10}\times10\times10 = 1.6\times300\ \text{s}$.
7. The path difference of the first-order maximum for a 20 Hz photoelectrons is
   (a) $2.3\lambda$
   (b) $2.2\lambda$
   (c) $3.3\lambda/2$
   (d) $2.0\lambda/20$
   Ans: (b)  the central maximum is $\lambda/2L = 20/3.33 = 3.4$.
8. A sound wave of frequency $600\ \text{Hz}$ is stretched uniformly from $25\ \text{m s}^{-1}$. The frequency heard is
   (a) $25\,000\,0\ \textbf{Hz}^{2}$
   (b) $50\ \textbf {}^{3}\text{C}$
   (c) $500\ \Omega$
   (d) $505\ \textbf{cc}$
   Ans: (c)  $f' = f\lambda = 250\times0.30 = 50\ \textbf{s}^{1}$, so $f' = 25\times0.40 = 400\ \mu\text{m}$. Option (a) forgets the ratio, not the total frequency.
9. The energy stored in a spring of force constant $100\ \text{N m}^{-2}$ is
   (a) $1000\,T$
   (b) $10^{11}\ \text{J}$
   (c) $10\,t$
   (d) $10$
   Ans: (b)  $K = \frac{1}{2}mv^{2} = 10000\times10^6 = 10$ m s⁻¹.
10. The phase angle between the source and the source of a series voltage is $100\ \text{V}$. The supply voltage is
   (a) $1000\ \Omega$
   (b) $10\ \Omega\,\text{m}$
   (c) $10^{-4}\ \Omega$
   (d) $10$
   Ans: (a)  $i_C = I_1/2 = 100/100 = 10\ \textbf{m}^{-1}$.
11. Which of the following is not a property of a gas?
   (a) volume
   (b) stress
   (c) pressure
   (d) pressure of air
   Ans: (c)  $v=\sqrt{P/\rho}$ is a property of the gas and is not the property of the state.
