[BLE|C8|computer-science | Number System]
Convert the binary number $11010_2$ to its decimal equivalent.
  A. $26_{10}$
  B. $27_{10}$
  C. $25_{10}$
  D. $28_{10}$
  key A — The binary number $11010_2$ converts to $26_{10}$ because $1*2^4 + 1*2^3 + 0*2^2 + 1*2^1 + 0*2^0 = 26$.

  B. Increasing the use of non-renewable resources to boost current economic activities
  C. Focusing solely on immediate economic benefits without regard to environmental consequences
  D. Prioritizing industrial growth over ecological sustainability

[IOE|BE|fundamental-of-electrical-an | General]
In a series RLC circuit at resonance, what happens to the impedance?
  A. It becomes zero
  B. It equals the resistance
  C. It reaches its maximum value
  D. It equals the inductive reactance
  key B — At resonance, the inductive and capacitive reactances cancel out, leaving only the resistance as the circuit's impedance.

  C. The entropy of an isolated system not in equilibrium will tend to increase over time.

[BLE|C8|mathematics | Statistics]
The standard deviation of a dataset is $4$. What is the variance of this dataset?
  A. $4$
  B. $16$
  C. $2$
  D. $8$
  key B — Variance is the square of the standard deviation, thus $4^2 = 16$.

[NEB|C11|chemistry | Organic Chemistry]
According to Zaitsev's rule, when an alkene is formed from an alcohol via dehydration, the product will be:
  A. The alkene with the most substituted double bond.
  B. The alkene with the least substituted double bond.
  C. The alkene with the highest boiling point.
  D. The alkene with the lowest boiling point.
  key A — Zaitsev's rule predicts the formation of the most substituted alkene during elimination reactions, leading to greater stability.
