## Answer key and marking scheme

1. (a) 70S - prokaryotic ribosomes are 70S (50S + 30S); 80S is eukaryotic.
2. (c) its moist vascular skin - cutaneous respiration through a moist skin richly supplied with capillaries; it must stay damp or it suffocates.
3. (d) the protection, restoration and sustainable management of biodiversity - it is an applied, goal-driven science: describe biodiversity, find why it is being lost, and act.
4. (b) locomotion from place to place - plants are living but do not move from place to place; all living things show the other three.
5. (a) cosmozoic or panspermia - the cosmozoic or panspermia theory of Richter and Arrhenius. It only moves the problem elsewhere.
6. (b) secondary or intermediate host - the mosquito is the primary host because the sexual cycle takes place in it; the human is secondary. This is inverted by most students.
7. (c) $8.33\times 10^{6}$ - The calculation directly provided in the content shows the division equals $8.33\times 10^{6}$.
8. (c) Stanley Miller and Harold Urey - Miller and Urey, 1953; they got glycine, alanine and aspartic acid in a week.
9. (a) magnesium - Mg²⁺ sits in the porphyrin ring; iron is in haemoglobin and cytochromes.
10. (d) rRNA - rRNA is transcribed and packed with protein in the nucleolus.
