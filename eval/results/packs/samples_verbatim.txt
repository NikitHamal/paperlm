[DC Circuits] MCQ <teacher>
Q. In a parallel circuit, what happens to the current at a junction where it splits into three branches?
  a) The voltage across each branch is divided equally among the branches.
  b) The current in each branch is the same as the incoming current.
  c) The total incoming current equals the sum of the currents in the three branches.
  d) The resistance in each branch adds up to the total resistance.
Key: c  Why: By Kirchhoff's first law, the total incoming current at a junction is equal to the sum of the currents flowing out of the junction.

[Reflection at curved mirror] 3-mark <teacher>
Q. Given a convex rear-view mirror with a radius of curvature of $2.0\ \text{m}$, explain how the angle $\theta$ and $2\theta$ can be approximated using the distances $PC$ and $PF$. Assume $N$ lies practically at the pole $P$.
Model answer: 1. The angle $\theta \approx \frac{h}{PC}$ where $PC \approx NC$. 2. Similarly, $2\theta \approx \frac{h}{PF}$ with $PF \approx NF$. 3. For a convex mirror with $R = 2.0\ \text{m}$, $F = R/2 = 1.0\ \text{m}$, thus approximations hold near pole $P$.

[Electric field] 3-mark <teacher>
Q. Define electric flux and write its formula when a flat area $A$ is placed in a uniform electric field $\vec{E}$ making an angle $\theta$ with the normal to the area. What are the SI units of electric flux?
Model answer: Electric flux measures field 'flow' through a surface. Formula: $\Phi_E = \vec{E}\cdot\vec{A} = EA\cos\theta$. SI units: N m² C⁻¹ or V m.

[Animal Tissues] 3-mark <teacher>
Q. Given an image magnification of 1000x, calculate the actual size in micrometers (μm) if the image size is 40 mm. Also, convert 2.4 cm into micrometers.
Model answer: Actual size: $\frac{40\ \text{mm}}{1000} = 0.040\ \text{mm} = 40\ \mu\text{m}$. Conversion: $2.4\ \text{cm} = 24\ \text{mm} = 24\,000\ \mu\text{m}$.

[Developmental Biology] 3-mark <teacher>
Q. Given that each secondary spermatocyte in a frog contains 13 chromosomes, each consisting of two chromatids, calculate the total number of chromatids present. Additionally, describe the shape and size of a frog's egg.
Model answer: Each secondary spermatocyte has 13 chromosomes, each with 2 chromatids, thus totaling $13 \times 2 = 26$ chromatids. A frog's egg is spherical with a diameter of 1.8 mm.
