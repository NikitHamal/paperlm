[Ecology] 3-mark <teacher>
Q. Explain the process of nitrification and how nitrogen is assimilated by plants and animals.
Model answer: Nitrosomonas converts $NH_4^+$ to $NO_2^-$, then Nitrobacter converts $NO_2^-$ to $NO_3^-$, absorbed by plants. Plants use $NO_3^-$ to synthesize amino acids and proteins. Animals obtain nitrogen by consuming these plant-based proteins.

[Evolutionary Biology] 3-mark <teacher>
Q. Explain how deviations from the Hardy–Weinberg equilibrium equation ($p^2 + 2pq + q^2 = 1$) indicate evolutionary changes in a population.
Model answer: 1. Hardy–Weinberg equilibrium assumes no mutation, migration, selection, and random mating in a large population. 2. Deviations from genotype frequencies $p^2$, $2pq$, $q^2$ suggest these conditions are not met. 3. Such deviations signify the population is evolving.
