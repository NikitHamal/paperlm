[Electric field] 3-mark <teacher>
Q. Explain how the electric field strength is determined using electric field lines and identify if there is any point between two like charges where the resultant electric field is zero.
Model answer: The electric field strength is determined by the density of field lines; more lines per unit area indicate a stronger field ($E$). For two like charges, the field lines between them point in the same direction, adding up and never cancelling out, hence there is no point where the resultant field is zero.
