Q. Calculate the temperature of a blackbody if the wavelength of maximum emission is $500\times10^{-9}$ m using Wien's displacement law.
Model answer: Using Wien's displacement law, $T = \frac{b}{\lambda_m}$. Given $b = 2.898\times10^{-3}$ m·K and $\lambda_m = 500\times10^{-9}$ m, the temperature is $T = \frac{2.898\times10^{-3}}{500\times10^{-9}} = 5.80\times10^{3}\ \text{K}$.
