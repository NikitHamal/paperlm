[Reflection at curved mirror] 5-mark <teacher>
Q. Explain how the position of an object relative to the focal point ($F$) and center of curvature ($C$) affects the type of image formed by a concave mirror, considering both real and virtual images. Use the mirror formula to support your explanation.
Model answer: The position of an object relative to $F$ and $C$ on a concave mirror determines the image type. When the object is beyond $C$, a real, inverted, and diminished image forms between $F$ and $C$. Between $C$ and $F$, the image is real, inverted, and magnified. At $F$, no image is formed as rays are parallel. Between $F$ and the pole, a virtual, upright, and magnified image is formed. The mirror formula $\frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{v} + \frac{1}{u}$ applies universally, where $v$ is negative for real images and positive for virtual ones.
