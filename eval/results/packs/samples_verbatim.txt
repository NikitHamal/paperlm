[Reflection at curved mirror] 3-mark <teacher>
Q. Given a concave spherical mirror with $u=-30\ \text{cm}$ and $f=-20\ \text{cm}$, calculate the image distance $v$. Use the mirror formula to show your calculations.
Model answer: Using the mirror formula $\dfrac1v=\dfrac1f-\dfrac1u$, substitute $f=-20\ \text{cm}$ and $u=-30\ \text{cm}$. This gives $\dfrac1v=-\dfrac1{20}+\dfrac1{30}=-\dfrac1{60}$. Therefore, $v=-60\ \text{cm}$. The image is formed at $-60\ \text{cm}$ from the mirror.
