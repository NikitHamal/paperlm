[Embryology] MCQ <gold>
Q. Which type of artificial vegetative propagation induces roots on a branch while it is still attached to the parent plant, and suits plants with soft branches lying near the soil?
  a) cutting
  b) grafting
  c) air layering
  d) soil layering
Key: d  Why: in simple (soil) layering the branch is bent into the soil while still attached and roots then form. Air layering works on upright branches, and grafting and cutting detach the piece at once.

[Developmental Biology] MCQ <gold>
Q. The cavity formed inside the embryo during gastrulation is the
  a) pseudocoel
  b) blastocoel
  c) neurocoel
  d) archenteron
Key: d  Why: the archenteron becomes the gut. Students confuse it with the blastocoel, which it gradually replaces.

[Plant Physiology] MCQ <gold>
Q. Which one of the following parts is not responsible for transpiration in plants?
  a) root
  b) leaves
  c) all aerial parts
  d) stem
Key: a  Why: transpiration is loss from aerial parts; roots absorb water. Option (d) is true of transpiration, not its opposite.

[Plant Anatomy] MCQ <teacher>
Q. In dicot stems, which structure allows for secondary growth by dividing between xylem and phloem?
  a) epidermis
  b) pith
  c) cortex
  d) cambium
Key: d  Why: The cambium is the strip of thin cells between xylem and phloem that enables secondary growth in dicots.

[Genetics] MCQ <teacher>
Q. How many different codons can be formed using the four nucleotide bases in mRNA?
  a) 16
  b) 48
  c) 64
  d) 256
Key: c  Why: With four bases, the number of possible codons is calculated as $4^{3} = 64$.

Q. The earthworm commonly used for vermicomposting is
  a) Pheretima posthuma
  b) Hirudinaria
  c) Eisenia fetida
  d) Lumbricus terrestris only

[Human Biology] MCQ <gold>
Q. The hormone that maintains the endometrium during pregnancy and is secreted by the corpus luteum is
  a) progesterone
  b) FSH
  c) oestrogen
  d) testosterone
Key: a  Why: progesterone from the corpus luteum (later the placenta). Oestrogen builds the endometrium in the first half of the cycle.

[Biotechnology] 3-mark <teacher>
Q. Given an initial amount of 5 target molecules, calculate the total number of copies produced after 30 cycles of PCR and state the final quantity.
Model answer: Each cycle doubles the target molecules. After 30 cycles, $2^{30} = 10^9$ copies are made per initial molecule. Starting with 5 molecules, the total is $N = 5 \times 2^{30} = 5.37 \times 10^9$ copies.

[Animal Tissues] 3-mark <teacher>
Q. Calculate the number of sarcomeres in a muscle fiber that is 24,000 μm long if each sarcomere is 2.4 μm. Then determine the new length if each sarcomere extends to 1.8 μm.
Model answer: Using $n = \frac{24\,000\ \mu\text{m}}{2.4\ \mu\text{m}} = 10\,000\ \text{sarcomeres}$, the initial count is 10,000. The new length is $10\,000 \times 1.8\ \mu\text{m} = 18\,000\ \mu\text{m} = 1.8\ \text{cm}$.
