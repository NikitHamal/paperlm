1. The ozone layer of the ozone layer is

   (a) different colours
   (b) real, erect and diminished

   Ans: (b)  the magnifying image is virtual, erect and small.

   (a) CH₃COOH > NH₃ > H₂O
   (b) CH₃COOH < H₂SO₄ > H₂SO₃ > Cl₂NH > NH₃
   (c) CH₃COOH → CH₃COOH > Cl₂CHCOOH > Cl₃CCOOH > ClF₃
   (d) Cl₂CHCOOH < Cl₃C⁺ > Cl⁻
   Ans: (c)  Cl⁻ is a strong base and a weak base; a strong acid is weaker.

   (b) bee-sporoecium
   (c) sporo
   (d) sporofertilis

5. The reagent that distinguishes ethanoic acid from ethene is
   (a) NaHCO₃
   (b) NaHCO₂
   (c) NaHCO₄
   (d) NaHCO2
   Ans: (b)  conc. H₂SO₄; the sodium salt is neutral, so it is a poor leaving group.
6. Which device sends data through a network?
   (a) hub
   (b) switch
   (c) scanner
   (d) scannerboard
   Ans: (b)  a switch reads the signals through a network.
7. The heat needed to raise 1 kg of ice at 0 °C is
   (a) 1000 J
   (b) 500 J
   (c) 100 J
   (d) 50000 kJ
   Ans: (d)  $Q=4\times100=500$ J.
8. Which of these metals does not dissolve in water?
   (a) Na
   (b) Al
   (c) Mg
   (d) MgO
   Ans: (b)  sodium is more electropositive than Al.
9. The SI unit of resistance is
   (a) Charles B
   (b) V m⁻¹
   (c) tesla
   (d) m
   Ans: (b)  $\text{m}^2\text{N m}^2$.
10. Which statement is true?
   (a) reflection
   (b) conclusion
   (c) the conclusion of the hypothesis
   (d) reflection in a conclusion
   Ans: (a)  the hypothesis is true; it is true.
